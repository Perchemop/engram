# Code of conduct

Engram is a small, spare-time project. Its community is meant to be a calm,
welcoming place to build and use free spaced-repetition software. This
document sets the behaviour we expect from everyone who takes part.

It is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, adapted to a one-maintainer project and kept short.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
Engram a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the project:

- Showing empathy and kindness towards other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or
  email address, without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or reviews.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions and commit messages, and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public.

## How to report a problem

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately. Do
not open a public issue about it.

- Use GitHub's **Report abuse** feature from the relevant comment, user or
  repository page; and
- email the maintainer at **conduct@example.com**.

The maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident. Reports are handled by the maintainer only, kept confidential, and
reviewed as promptly as spare-time capacity allows (expect a reply within
seven days). A reporter will not be asked to confront the person involved.

## Enforcement and consequences

The maintainer will investigate every report and apply any action they deem
appropriate to the circumstances. Consequences escalate with severity and
repetition, and include:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning that explains the nature of the
   violation and why the behaviour was inappropriate, with a request to stop.
   A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, such as
   no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction with the project
   and its community for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from any interaction within the
   project.

Breaches of a ban may lead to a longer or permanent ban. The maintainer may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and will communicate the reasons
when appropriate.
